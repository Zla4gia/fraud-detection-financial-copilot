# Disputing a charge

Start by checking your statement. A charge with an unfamiliar merchant name is sometimes a legitimate purchase under the business's trading name, a subscription, or a family member's purchase. Look at the date and the amount and check your own receipts and emails.

If the charge is wrong, first try the merchant when the problem is a billing mistake, a duplicate charge or a refund that did not arrive. If that does not solve it, file a dispute with your card issuer. Disputes usually have a time limit that starts at the statement date, so do not wait.

Give the issuer the details in writing and keep copies of everything: receipts, emails, screenshots and the dates of your calls. Ask what happens to the amount while the dispute is open, because the practice differs between issuers and card types.
