# Card security basics

Turn on transaction alerts in your banking app so that you see every payment quickly. Many apps also let you lock or freeze a card temporarily. A lock is a useful first step when you are unsure about a payment, while you wait for the fraud team.

Review your statements regularly, for example once a week, and look at small unfamiliar charges too: fraudsters sometimes test a card with a small amount before a larger one.

Use a different password for every financial site, and use a password manager if you can. Be careful with saving card details on websites you do not know well. If your issuer offers virtual or single-use card numbers for online shopping, they limit the damage when a merchant is breached.
