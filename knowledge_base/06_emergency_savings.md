# Emergency savings

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected costs such as a repair, a medical bill or a period without income. A common rule of thumb is to aim for three to six months of essential expenses, but the right size depends on how stable your income is and how many people depend on it.

If that target feels far away, start smaller: a first goal of one month of expenses, or a fixed amount from each payday, is already a cushion. Keep the money somewhere you can reach quickly and that is separate from everyday spending, so that you are not tempted to use it.

After you use the fund, rebuild it before adding other goals. This is general education; for decisions about your own situation, talk to a qualified adviser.
