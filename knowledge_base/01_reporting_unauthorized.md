# Reporting an unauthorized transaction

If you see a card transaction that you did not make, contact your card issuer's fraud department as soon as possible. Use the phone number on the back of your card or inside your banking app. Do not use a phone number or link from an email or text message.

Tell them the date, the amount and the merchant name exactly as shown on your statement. Ask them to block or replace the card and to open a fraud case. Write down the date, the name of the person you spoke to and any case number you are given.

Report quickly. The rules that limit what a customer can be asked to pay for unauthorized card use depend on the country, on the type of card (credit and debit cards are often treated differently) and on how fast the loss is reported. Check the current rules for your situation on your regulator's website.
