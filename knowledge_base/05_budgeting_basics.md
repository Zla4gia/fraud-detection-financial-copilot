# Budgeting basics

Budgeting starts with seeing where money goes. Group spending into categories such as groceries, dining, transport, shopping, utilities and entertainment, and look at the share each category takes of the total.

A popular rule of thumb is 50/30/20: about half of take-home pay for needs, about 30 percent for wants and about 20 percent for savings and paying down debt. It is a rough starting point, not a law; your own goals, housing costs and income decide what is reasonable.

Do not judge a budget from a few days of spending. Rent, bills and subscriptions arrive on different dates, so look at at least a full month. Review recurring charges regularly and cancel what you no longer use. "Too much" on a category only has a meaning compared with your own plan.
